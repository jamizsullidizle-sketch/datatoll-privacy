DataToll Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026 · Applies to: the DataToll Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 and later · Contact: Lettersmakenumbers001@gmail.com

Summary

DataToll does not send any of your data anywhere. Everything it records stays in your browser's local extension storage on your device. DataToll has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no accounts. The developer never receives your data.

What DataToll handles (on your device only)

DataToll records nothing until you agree to an in-product disclosure ("I agree, start metering"). After that:

  • Browsing activity (web history): when a page you visit makes a network request to a domain on DataToll's built-in tracker list, DataToll records the tracker hostname, the company that owns it, the website where it happened (domain only, never the full URL or path), the request type (script, pixel, etc.), whether you blocked it, and the time. Random per-user labels in tracker hostnames are masked.
  • Settings: which companies you chose to block, whether metering is paused, and when you gave consent.
  • Optional personal details: if you type your name, email address, state/country or identifiers into the privacy-request generator, they are saved locally so you don't have to retype them.

DataToll does not read page content, form data, cookies, passwords, request bodies, or full URLs, and it does not inject scripts into websites.

How it is used

Only to provide DataToll's features to you: the ledger, the toolbar popup, per-company blocking, exports, the share card, and the text of privacy-request drafts. The "toll" is a symbolic, for-awareness metric, not a legal debt.

Sharing and transfers

None. DataToll does not sell, transfer, or share your data with the developer or any third party, and it does not use it for advertising, credit-worthiness, or lending. Extension pages enforce a Content Security Policy that blocks network connections (connect-src 'none').

DataToll never sends privacy requests for you. If you choose "Open in my email app", your own mail program opens with a draft. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Exports (CSV/JSON) and the share image are saved only where you choose. The share image shows totals and company names, not your browsing history.

Retention and your choices

  • The event log keeps the most recent 3,000 events. Summary totals are kept until you reset them.
  • Pause stops recording at any time.
  • Reset on the dashboard erases the ledger and event log.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

Security

Data stays inside Chrome's extension storage on your device, protected by your operating system account and Chrome's profile security. It is never transmitted.

Children

DataToll is not directed at children under 13.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
